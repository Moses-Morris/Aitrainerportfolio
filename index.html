I'm a software and data engineer with 8+ years of experience, and the founder of CloserTune Agency Ltd, a small team building web platforms, data infrastructure, and AI evaluation systems for clients across software, data, and branding work.

My core stack spans Python (Django, Flask, FastAPI), Go, Node.js, TypeScript, and PHP/Laravel on the backend, paired with React, Vue, Angular, and Next.js on the front end — all backed by PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker, and AWS. On the data side, I build ETL pipelines and Power BI dashboards that turn operational noise into decisions people can act on.

Alongside engineering, I hold active contracts with Vetto Arena and Welo Data evaluating and red-teaming AI model outputs — rubric-based scoring, multi-turn conversation annotation, and QA auditing that keeps me close to how these systems actually fail. I also carry WCAG Level AAA accessibility experience, auditing and remediating products and writing VPATs for procurement, and I've spent four years live-streaming IT and AI topics on TikTok and Facebook — so explaining complex systems clearly is second nature, not an afterthought.

Whatever the discipline, the throughline is the same: understand the real problem, build something that works under pressure, and leave behind documentation good enough that someone else can pick it up without calling me.
